# Pictures of the cat

Generated with AI image tools for this game, then cut out and framed
(transparent background, 640 px wide, WebP).

| File | Made with |
|---|---|
| ginger-adult.webp | ChatGPT |
| ginger-kitten.webp | ChatGPT |
| ginger-old.webp | Gemini |
| ginger-sleep.webp | Gemini |
| ginger-walk-1/2.webp | Gemini |
| black-*.webp | Gemini (black-sleep made darker to match) |
| grey-tabby-*.webp | Gemini (on a green background) |
| white-*.webp | Gemini (on a green background) |
| calico-*.webp | Gemini (on a green background) |
| tuxedo-*.webp | Gemini (on a green background) |
| persian-*.webp, mainecoon-*.webp, bobtail-*.webp | Gemini (on a green background; each walk is one picture with the cat twice) |

The white and calico walks are also one picture with the cat twice.

To add a coat: make the same pictures (sitting adult, old, asleep, two
steps of a walk to the right, kitten), cut them out with tools/cutout.py
(`split` cuts a picture with the cat twice into the two steps), and add
them here and to CAT_PICTURES in art.js.
