# Pictures of the cat

Generated with AI image tools for this game, then cut out and framed
(transparent background, 640 px wide, WebP).

| File | Made with |
|---|---|
| ginger-adult.webp | ChatGPT |
| ginger-kitten.webp | ChatGPT |
| ginger-old.webp | Gemini |
| ginger-sleep.webp | Gemini |
| ginger-walk.webp | Gemini |
| black-*.webp | Gemini (black-sleep made darker to match) |
| grey-tabby-*.webp | Gemini (on a green background) |

To add a coat: make the same pictures (sitting adult, old, asleep, two
steps of a walk to the right, kitten), cut them out with tools/cutout.py,
and add them here and to CAT_PICTURES in art.js.
